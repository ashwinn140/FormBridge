Air India PDA Statement → ERP Purchase Upload Converter

Converts the Air India (AITSL) PDA/CASS running-account statement into the Logi-Sys ERP "Purchase Upload" format — matching each charge line to its job number, splitting out CGST/SGST from the GST-inclusive amount, and classifying Export Terminal Handling, CCL Terminal Handling and Import Delivery-Order charges automatically. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1 Upload the PDA Statement

The Air India "PDA Statement" Excel export (the file with Bill No / Invoice No / Invoice Type / AWB No / DR / CR columns). PDF copies of the same statement aren't parsed — use the Excel version.

2 Upload Job / AWB register(s)

One or more Excel registers that map an AWB number to a Logi-Sys Job No (shipment, console, or CCL job). Add as many as you need (e.g. NG and NCS registers) — matching tries them in the order listed. Matching is done on the last 8 characters of the AWB, so dashes/spacing/prefix differences don't matter.

3 Mapping & constants editable

Edit anything here before converting — these values are read fresh each time you click Convert.

Invoice-type → charge mapping

Which ERP charge, cost centre, LOB and Ref Type to use for each Air India "Invoice Type" value. Rows with an Invoice Type not listed here are skipped and reported as "unmapped type".

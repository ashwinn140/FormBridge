Summary

Pricing quotes

Not loaded

The RFQ number comes from the shipment register (the RFQ No on each house) and is looked up in the pricing sheet (INQ NO). A master with several houses is matched house by house, and the quoted amounts are added up over its houses. The quoted buy and sell then sit next to what CASS billed.

Matching

Pricing team index

RFQ number prefix and the pricing person. Anyone can add a new prefix.

 

Actual billing

Not loaded

The job profit report gives what was billed (income) and booked (expense) on each job, by charge head. Each AWB is linked through the AWB on the report, or through the register's shipment and consol jobs, and the charge heads ticked at the top of the page are added up. New Globe's and NCS's reports stay separate, so you can see each company's share.

Purchase upload

Ready when lines are matched

One line per AWB under each airline invoice, in the ERP purchase template. AWBs that have no job are left out and listed below. AWBs that belong to the other company get their own provision entries underneath, once that company’s register is loaded.
